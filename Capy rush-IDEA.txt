Game concept: Capy Rush

Speed is secondary. The main hook is a "ripen and rush" loop: you raise valuable capybaras on a timer, and every few minutes a server-wide market event pays a huge bonus to anyone who can deliver one in time. I haven't searched Roblox for anything similar, so check for close matches before you commit.

Primary mechanic: ripening and the Market Rush
Raise: hatch baby capybaras in your pen. They ripen over real time (minutes to hours by rarity), so you earn while away.
Mutate: each capybara can roll a random mutation on ripening (golden, cosmic, and so on), which changes its value and its look. This is the variable reward that makes people check back.
Glow: a fully ripe rare capybara glows with a beam visible to the whole server. It's a status flex and a "look what I have" moment.
Market Rush: every 5–10 minutes a server-wide event spawns a far-off buyer paying 5x for 60 seconds. Everyone with a ripe capybara sprints to deliver it through the market street.
Cash out and reinvest: payouts buy better eggs, more pen slots, and speed upgrades.
Where speed comes in (secondary)
Your speed stat decides how fast you cross the obstacle street, so faster players make the deadline with more capybaras.
Speed is trained on treadmills in your pen while you wait for ripening, which fills the idle time.
Obstacles are speed-gated, so a lot of speed pays off at the moment that matters.
Why it should hook people
Idle income gives a reason to come back.
Random mutations give a reason to check in.
Scheduled server-wide events create a "don't miss it" pull every few minutes and give streamers a moment to build content around.
Visible glow turns your rare capybara into a status symbol.
A payoff moment where speed matters gives the grind an active climax, not just waiting.
Player interaction without stealing
Everyone rushes together, so the race is the social layer.
Optional: a slowing mud puddle or a bump that makes rivals drop pace briefly, with no item loss, which keeps new players from being griefed.
Monetization
Egg luck boosts, extra pen slots, and auto-collect.
A speed-boost pass and a "Rush skip" that lets you deliver from anywhere once per event.
Cosmetic auras and trails for glowing capybaras.
Risks
The timers-plus-mutations format is the same shape as Grow a Garden. The Market Rush is what makes this different, so it has to feel great in the first session.
Event timing matters. If a rush is too rare, players leave, and if it's too frequent, it feels like a chore. Tune this in testing.
Server-side validation is a must. Ripening timers, mutation rolls, and deliveries all need to be server-authoritative so nobody can duplicate capybaras.
