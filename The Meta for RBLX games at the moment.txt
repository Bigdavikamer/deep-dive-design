Core loop template
Run on a treadmill (or hold a key to run) and earn Steps or Energy.
Spend it on speed upgrades and better treadmills.
Faster speed means higher income and access to new zones.
Rebirth to reset with a permanent multiplier.
Add a steal, defend, or race layer so other players affect you.
Ideas

1. Treadmill Tycoon: Steal a Runner

You own a base with treadmill slots, and each slot holds a pet or character that runs and produces income.
Runners are obtained from eggs or a conveyor belt with rarity tiers.
Other players can sneak in and steal your best runner. You defend with speed-boosted traps or a lockdown timer.
Hook: an exposed rare runner with a countdown creates tension and stories.

2. Escape the Gym

A tall tower or obstacle course is chased by a rising hazard (lava, a giant angry trainer, a rising wave).
You run on treadmills between sections to charge a speed meter that powers your sprint and jumps.
Each run earns coins, which buy permanent speed, a stamina bar, and trails.
Hook: short 2-3 minute runs, instant retry, and a "so close" feeling on failure.

3. Run to Grow

Every step gives size or "power", and your character visibly gets bigger and faster.
Bigger characters can smash through walls or pass gates locked behind speed thresholds.
Zones unlock by speed (Forest → Desert → Space).
Hook: constant visible progress and gated areas you can see but can't reach yet.

4. Race the Server

Treadmills feed a live leaderboard, and the top runner each round gets a big bonus and a crown.
Rounds last 5 minutes, and the winner picks a server-wide modifier (low gravity, double coins, etc.).
Hook: competition plus server-wide events people want to be part of.

5. Treadmill Pet Farm

Hatch pets that run on their own treadmills while you're active and while you're away (offline earnings).
Merge duplicates into stronger versions.
Daily login gives a free egg, and a timed "hot" treadmill boosts income.
Hook: collection plus return-tomorrow mechanics.

6. Sprint Heist

You run through a mansion or vault, and speed pads and treadmills carry you faster to the loot.
Guards get faster each wave, so you upgrade your speed to keep up.
Loot is banked at the exit, and dying loses part of it.
Hook: risk vs. reward on every run.
What keeps players hooked
Fast first reward: the first upgrade should come within 30 seconds.
Visible progress: trails, auras, and bigger characters, so upgrades are noticeable.
Rebirth or prestige: gives long-term goals.
Limited-time events: double speed weekends, boss runs, and holiday treadmills.
Monetization that fits: 2x speed pass, auto-run, extra pet slots, and premium trails. Avoid making the free path feel slow to the point of frustration.
